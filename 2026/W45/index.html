Dependency maintenance marathon across 20+ repos with splashboard glibc fix

58 reviews, 11 PRs merged, and 5 releases shipped in a week dominated by Dependabot wrangling and a notable sysinfo compatibility fix.

This week was a pure dependency maintenance sprint — the kind of week where you're the gardener of a large ecosystem, pruning and updating across more than 20 repositories simultaneously. From Rust crates like tree-sitter-scala and log, to JavaScript packages like glob, eslint, and @mui/x-tree-view, to Ruby gems like nokogiri and addressable, the breadth of the update sweep was impressive. mdts alone absorbed 11 PRs this week, making it the clear center of gravity.

Alongside that, the LiteLLM pricing sync automation continued running, keeping splashboard's LLM cost data fresh without manual intervention.

Five releases shipped across mdts, splashboard, cycle-flyer, DroidFlyer, and Norimaki. sysinfo 0.40 breaks glibc 2.17 builds in splashboard
The splashboard bump from sysinfo 0.39 to 0.40 quietly pulled in GPU feature flags that depend on newer glibc symbols, breaking builds targeting glibc 2.17. The fix was surgical: disable the unused sysinfo features at the Cargo level to restore compatibility without losing any actual functionality. This shipped as v2.10.4 with a clear commit trail — 'fix(deps): disable unused sysinfo features to keep glibc 2.17 builds'. It's a good reminder that even patch-level Rust crate bumps can have non-obvious downstream ABI implications.
